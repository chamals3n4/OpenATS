OpenATS 1.0 is out today. It's a free and open-source hiring platform that you can run yourself. You get everything you need to hire in one place, and your candidate data stays with you.

Here's the story behind it.

{/* truncate */}

## How it started

Last year I was working with an organization that was hiring for a few roles. They were handling it all with email and spreadsheets. CVs were in someone's inbox, interview feedback was in chats, and tracking who was at which stage took a lot of manual work.

I checked a few hiring tools for them. Most were priced per user per month, which was too much for them. They also didn't want their candidates' CVs and personal details stored on another company's servers.

So I built a small internal hiring system for them instead.

## Why I made it open source

When I talked about it with friends who run startups and work at other organizations, I found out many of them had the same problem. They wanted a proper hiring tool, but either couldn't afford one or needed to keep their data on their own servers.

So I decided to build a new version from scratch and make it open source. Anyone can use it for free, host it on their own server, and change it if they need to.

That's OpenATS, and after months of work it's ready for real use.

## What you can do with it

OpenATS handles the full hiring flow, from posting a job to sending the offer:

- Post jobs with department, salary and your own questions
- Show them on a careers page, or add them to your own website
- Get applications and search through all the CVs
- Move candidates through your hiring stages with drag and drop
- Send assessments to candidates automatically
- Send take-home assignments and get the work back in one place
- Schedule interviews with Google Calendar and Meet, and fill in scorecards
- Rate candidates and discuss them with your team
- See a score for each candidate based on answers, assessments, ratings and interviews
- Send offer letters that candidates can accept or decline online
- Send emails to candidates using templates
- Get notified when a candidate applies, books an interview or answers an offer
- Give admins, hiring managers and interviewers different access

About AI: OpenATS can write short notes on a CV if you turn it on. It's off by default and it doesn't affect the score. I talked with some people who work in recruitment, and they told me AI shouldn't decide who gets hired. I agree with that. The score comes from what your team actually checked.

## Get started
