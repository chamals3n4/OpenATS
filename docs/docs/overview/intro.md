# What is OpenATS ?

OpenATS is an open-source applicant tracking system designed to help teams manage their entire hiring process in one place. It allows you to organize candidates, track their progress through different stages, and collaborate with your team more effectively. Whether you're a startup or a growing company, OpenATS helps you build a structured, simple, and efficient recruitment workflow without relying on expensive tools.

## Why OpenATS Exists ?

Hiring today can quickly become messy, especially when candidates, feedback, and communication are scattered across emails, spreadsheets, and different tools. OpenATS brings everything into one place, making the process more organized, transparent, and easy to manage.

Most companies rely on proprietary ATS platforms. While these tools are powerful, they often come with high costs, limited customization, and less control over your own data. For many startups and growing teams, this makes it harder to build a flexible and scalable hiring process.

In many areas of software, modern open-source alternatives have emerged to replace expensive proprietary tools. However, in the ATS space, there are currently no truly modern, flexible, and developer-friendly open-source solutions available. OpenATS is built to fill that gap.

It provides a simple, structured, and open approach to managing hiring. Teams can organize candidates, track their progress through different stages, and collaborate more effectively, all within a single system.

- Keep all candidates and hiring data in one place
- Track progress through clear recruitment stages
- Collaborate with your team more effectively
- Avoid scattered tools like spreadsheets and emails
- Customize and extend the system based on your needs
- Maintain full control over your data with an open-source solution
- Use a cost-effective alternative to expensive proprietary ATS platforms

OpenATS is designed to help teams build better hiring workflows while giving them the flexibility and ownership that modern tools should provide.

## Core Concepts & Features

OpenATS is built around a simple and structured hiring workflow, combining key concepts and features into one system.

- Pipelines & Stages – Structure your hiring process with clear steps
- Candidate Management – Keep all candidate information in one place
- Progress Tracking – Easily see where each candidate stands
- Team Collaboration – Share feedback and work together
- Custom Workflows – Adapt the system to your hiring style
- Centralized System – Replace scattered tools with one platform
