# Find Your Way Around the Dashboard

After you sign in, you land on **Overview**. It's a good page to check each morning: it shows how hiring is going and what's waiting for you.

{/* SCREENSHOT: overview/overview-full.png - The whole Overview page as a Super Admin, with the sidebar, key figures, charts and attention panel */}

## What's in the sidebar

- **Overview** is your hiring summary.
- **Manage Jobs** is where you create and run jobs.
- **Candidates** lists everyone who has applied.
- **Interviews** shows your interview calendar.
- **Assessments** holds your tests and take-home assignments.
- **Manage Offers** lists every offer. Interviewers don't see this one.
- **Templates** holds your emails and interview types.
- **Settings** covers your company, profile, careers page, users and integrations. You'll only see the parts your role can use.

## Reading the numbers

Use the two menus at the top to pick a period (the last 7, 30 or 90 days) and a department. All the figures update to match.

- **New applications** is how many people applied in that period.
- **Open positions** is how many jobs are published right now.
- **Time to hire** is the average number of days from applying to accepting an offer.
- **Offer acceptance** is the percentage of offers sent in that period that were accepted.

Below them, the **Applications and hires** chart shows whether interest is turning into actual hires. The pipeline chart shows how many people are at each stage, so if a stage is piling up, you'll see it here.

Hiring Managers only see numbers for their own jobs.

## See what needs you today

The attention panel is the most useful part of the page. It lists candidates who've been stuck in a stage for several days, offers still waiting for an answer, this week's interviews, and anyone who applied in the last 24 hours.

## Export a report

Need the numbers in a spreadsheet? Select **Export report** and choose **CSV**, which opens in Excel or Google Sheets. There's also **JSON** if a developer needs the data.

{/* SCREENSHOT: overview/export-report.png - The export dialog with CSV and JSON options */}

## If you're an Interviewer

Your Overview is simpler. **Needs you today** shows your interviews today, your interviews over the next 7 days, and any scorecards you still owe.
