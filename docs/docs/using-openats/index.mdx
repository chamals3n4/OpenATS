# How to Use OpenATS

New to OpenATS? Start at step 1 and work your way down. By step 13 you'll have taken a role from a blank job form to a signed offer. Already using it and just need one thing? Jump to that card.

This guide assumes OpenATS is already installed. If it isn't, follow the [Quick Start](../getting-started/make-setup) first.

## How hiring works here

Here's the short version. A Super Admin sets up the company and invites the team. A Hiring Manager gets the reusable pieces ready, such as email templates and tests, then creates a job and publishes it. Candidates apply from your careers page. Your team reviews them, moves them along, and interviews the best ones. The person you choose gets an offer, and everyone else gets a clear no.
