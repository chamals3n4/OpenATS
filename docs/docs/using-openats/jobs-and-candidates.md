# Jobs and candidates

OpenATS organizes recruitment around jobs and the candidates applying to them. Start by creating a job, then add or review candidates as they enter the hiring process.

## Create a job

Add the job title, department, description, requirements, and the people responsible for hiring. The person who creates the job is added to its hiring team automatically. Add other Hiring Managers and Interviewers when they need access to that job.

## Follow a candidate

Every candidate has one record containing their contact details, resume, applications, notes, interview feedback, and activity history. Keep decisions and discussion on this record so the hiring team does not have to search across email or spreadsheets.

## Move candidates through the pipeline

New installations include five stages: **Applied**, **Screening**, **Interviewed**, **Offer**, and **Rejected**. Move a candidate when their status changes. A consistent pipeline gives the team an accurate view of each job and makes delayed candidates easier to spot.

## A simple first workflow

1. Create a job and add the hiring team.
2. Add a candidate or receive an application through the career page.
3. Review the resume and move the candidate from Applied to Screening.
4. Schedule an interview and record feedback.
5. Move the candidate to Offer or Rejected when the team reaches a decision.
