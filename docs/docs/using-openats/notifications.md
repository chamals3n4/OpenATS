# Notifications

The bell at the top of the dashboard shows what has happened since you last looked. A number on it counts the notifications you haven't read.

## What you're told about

| Notification | When |
| --- | --- |
| **New application** | A candidate applies for a job |
| **Interview booked** | An interview gets its time, picked by the candidate or set by your team |
| **No interview time picked** | A candidate's link to pick a time ran out before they chose one. Send new times or set one yourself |
| **Assessment completed** | A candidate finishes an assessment |
| **Assignment handed in** | A candidate hands in a take-home assignment |
| **Offer accepted** / **Offer declined** | A candidate answers an offer |
| **Email not delivered** | An email to a candidate couldn't be sent. Check the candidate's email history and send it again |

## Who gets them

Notifications about a candidate go to the **hiring team** of their job. The interviewer of an interview is told about it too, even if they aren't on the team. You aren't told about something you did yourself.

An **Email not delivered** notification goes to the person who sent the email. If OpenATS sent it automatically, it goes to the hiring team.

You only see notifications for jobs you can still see. If you're taken off a job's hiring team, its notifications disappear from your list.

## Reading them

Click a notification to open the candidate, at the part of their page it's about. This marks it as read. Use **Mark all as read** to clear the count, and the **Unread** tab to see only the ones you haven't opened.

New notifications arrive while you have the dashboard open, without reloading the page.

The interviewer is also emailed when an interview with them gets its time.
